Privacy Policy — Era XI: Cricket Draft

Effective date: 17 September 2026  |  Last updated: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Beeshaker ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Era XI: Cricket Draft (the "App"). It also explains the choices and rights available to you.

1. Summary

  • You do not need to create an account to use the App.
  • Your drafts, auctions, matches, tournament results, achievements, settings, and other game progress are stored locally on your device. We do not receive that game data on our own servers.
  • The Android and iOS versions use Google AdMob to provide rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Google and participating advertising partners may automatically collect device, advertising, interaction, diagnostic, and approximate-location information as described below.
  • Where required, the App asks for your advertising and privacy choices before requesting ads. You can revisit applicable advertising privacy choices from the App's Privacy & Ad Choices control.
  • You can erase locally stored game data by using Reset Local Data in the App or by uninstalling the App.

2. Information stored locally by the App

The App stores information needed to provide and preserve your game experience, including:

  • draft and auction progress;
  • selected players, squads, formations, and game settings;
  • simulated matches, tournament results, trophies, and achievements;
  • momentum-spin activity and other game-event history; and
  • consent and preference information stored by the advertising consent tools.

This information is stored in a local database on your device (or in browser storage when you use the web version). The game does not require a user account, and we do not upload this gameplay information to a developer-operated server.

3. Information processed for advertising

The Android and iOS versions of the App use the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform. Depending on your device, region, settings, and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive:

  • your Internet Protocol (IP) address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and app identifiers, such as an advertising identifier, app-set identifier, or app/developer-scoped identifier;
  • advertising information, such as ads shown and whether an ad was viewed or interacted with;
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views;
  • device, browser, operating-system, language, and app-version information; and
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, crashes, hangs, and energy usage.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. More information about Google's processing is available in:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

We may receive aggregated advertising reports from Google, such as ad impressions, reward completions, estimated revenue, and broad performance information. These reports are used to operate and evaluate the App's advertising. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or precise location for advertising.

The web version does not use the Google Mobile Ads SDK unless this policy is updated to say otherwise.

4. Personalized, non-personalized, and limited ads

Depending on your region and choices, the App may request personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads:

  • Personalized ads may be selected using information about your interests or previous activity, where you have permitted this processing.
  • Non-personalized ads are selected using contextual information rather than a profile of your previous activity. Advertising providers may still use identifiers or local storage for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting, security, and fraud prevention where permitted.
  • Limited ads restrict personalization and certain uses of local identifiers.

In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other regions where consent is required, the App uses Google's consent platform to present the applicable choices before requesting ads. You may change available choices later through Privacy & Ad Choices in the App.

On iOS, the App may also ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing the advertising identifier or tracking activity across other companies' apps and websites. Declining that permission does not prevent you from using the App; ads may still be shown without access to the iOS advertising identifier.

5. How information is used

Information described in this policy may be used to:

  • provide, save, and restore App features and game progress;
  • display rewarded and interstitial advertisements;
  • record that a rewarded-ad condition was completed and provide the associated in-game benefit;
  • measure advertising delivery and performance;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, invalid advertising activity, and security incidents;
  • diagnose SDK or App performance problems; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms and policies.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing is carried out with your consent, to provide the App or a feature you requested, to comply with law, or for legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention, and operating and improving the App, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.

6. When information is shared

We do not sell your locally stored gameplay data. Information may be disclosed in the following circumstances:

  • Advertising and consent providers. Google and participating advertising partners process information to deliver, measure, secure, and improve ads, subject to your applicable choices and their own privacy terms.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or enforceable governmental requests, or to protect rights, safety, security, and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Business changes. If the App or its operator is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, relevant information may be transferred subject to appropriate protections and this policy.

Some privacy laws define targeted advertising disclosures as a "sale" or "sharing" even when no money is exchanged. Where those laws apply, you can use Privacy & Ad Choices in the App to exercise the choices made available for advertising processing.

7. Retention and deletion

Gameplay information remains on your device until you use Reset Local Data, clear the App's storage through your device or browser settings, or uninstall the App. Because we do not operate an account system or receive this local game database, we cannot remotely access or delete it for you.

Google and its advertising partners retain information according to their own policies, legal obligations, consent signals, and product settings. You can use the privacy controls described below or contact us with questions about the processing associated with the App.

We retain any information that you send directly to us, such as a support email, only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or comply with law.

8. Your privacy choices

Depending on your device and region, you can:

  • open Privacy & Ad Choices in the App to review or change choices offered through Google's consent platform;
  • control iOS tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking;
  • manage or delete your advertising identifier and related ad-privacy settings through your Android device settings;
  • reset locally stored game data using Reset Local Data in the App;
  • clear the App's storage or uninstall the App; and
  • contact us using the details below.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or a copy of personal information; object to or opt out of certain processing, targeted advertising, sale, or sharing; withdraw consent; appeal a denied privacy request; and complain to a competent data-protection authority. These rights may be subject to exceptions under applicable law.

Because the App has no accounts and we generally do not receive the locally stored game data, we may be unable to identify that data as relating to you or fulfil a request for data that remains solely on your device. We will respond to verifiable requests concerning information that we control as required by applicable law.

9. International processing

Google and its advertising partners may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, the relevant service providers use recognized safeguards for international transfers. Please review Google's privacy documentation for more information about its transfer practices.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled through the App. Local gameplay information remains within the App's device storage unless you choose to expose it through device-level backup, sharing, or similar operating-system features. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age that may apply in a user's jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account or developer-operated backend. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at marketitkenya@gmail.com.

If the App's intended audience changes to include children, we will update the App's advertising configuration, store disclosures, consent experience, and this policy before making that change.
